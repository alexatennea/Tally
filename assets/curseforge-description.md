# Tally

**How long until I level?** Tally answers that at a glance, from the XP you've actually been earning, and keeps your professions, gold and honor in the same place.

Built for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

## Features

- **Time to level** based on your recent XP rate. Choose the window it uses: the last 5 min, 10 min, 30 min, 1 hour, or your whole session.
- **XP bar** with XP to go, % through the level and **kills to level** (from the average of your last 5 kills). Rested XP is shown in purple.
- **Session stats**: XP gained and XP per hour. Hover for session time, session average, kills and average XP per kill.
- **Sessions that survive /reload** and short logouts. Time logged out isn't counted, and the session resets itself after 30 minutes without XP.
- **Professions**: your current and max rank for each primary and/or secondary profession. **Click one to open it.**
- **Gold**: your current gold, how much you've made (or spent) this session, and gold per hour.
- **Honor**: PvP rank progress where the game provides it, otherwise your honor points.

## Three ways to show it

- **Floating panel**: a small box you can drag anywhere.
- **Bar across the top** or **bar across the bottom**: everything on one line along the edge of the screen, with a thin XP line along its edge. The bar moves the default UI out of its way (the minimap and buffs for a top bar; action bars, menu and bags for a bottom bar), and puts it all back if you switch or hide the bar. You can turn that off in options.

## Getting around

- **Minimap button**: left-click to show or hide Tally, right-click for options, drag to move it.
- **Options page** under Options > AddOns > Tally to choose the display style and what's shown.

## Commands

- `/tally`: open options
- `/tally panel`, `/tally bar`, `/tally bottom`: switch display style
- `/tally reset`: start a new session
- `/tally show`, `/tally hide`, `/tally toggle`: show or hide Tally
- `/tally lock`: lock or unlock the floating panel's position
- `/tally center`: move the floating panel back to the middle of the screen
